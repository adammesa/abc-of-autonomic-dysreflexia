## Symptoms

- Pounding headache
- Nasal congestion
- Chest palpitations
- Stomach contraction
- Penile erection
- Paresthesia
- Anxiety

## Signs on Physical Exam
- Dilated pupils
- Flushing of skin above level of injury
- Pallor (cool extremities below level of injury)
- Piloerection
- Hyperhydrosis above the level of injury
- Bradycardia/arrhythmia (though tachycardia may occur)

## Investigations
If suspecting complications of AD, further investigations can be ordered as seen in Hypertensive Emergency (e.g. CT head for focal neurologic symptoms).

* Blood pressure BP greater than 20 mmHg above their usual baseline* (15 mmHg in children). BP should be monitored frequently (e.g. every 2-5 minutes) depending on severity.
