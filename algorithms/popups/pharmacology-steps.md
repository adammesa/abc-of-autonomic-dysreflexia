**Pharmacology**
1. CCB (i.e. Nifedipine 10 mg SL; onset <30 min)
2. ACEi (i.e. Captopril 12.5-25 mg SL/PO; onset <10 min SL, <30 min PO)
3. Nitrates (e.g. Nitroglycerin spray, transdermal, or if severe, IV)
    - Spread thin layer of 2% Nitroglycerin ointment 1.25-5cm topically on dry, hair-free site on chest or back
4. α-blocker (i.e. Prazosin 1 mg PO)
